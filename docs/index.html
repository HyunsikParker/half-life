When will you forget this?

Flashcard apps schedule reviews with a fixed rule: get it right, wait twice as long. But how fast a thing fades depends on the thing and on you. This model learned that decay from 10.2 million real reviews, and was then tested on 1.3 million more from learners it had never seen. It schedules from what it learned.

Does the learned schedule actually predict better?

Held out by learner — no person's reviews appear in both training and test data, so nothing is scored on someone the model has already met. Lower error is better.
